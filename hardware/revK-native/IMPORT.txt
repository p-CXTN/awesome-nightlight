NIGHTLIGHT REV K

LAYOUT
52 x 52 x 1.6 mm square carrier, two copper layers.
All three modules and every resistor, capacitor, MOSFET and LED mount on the
front. The USB-A connector is the only rear-mounted component.
Coordinates below are front views, with the three LEDs along the bottom edge.
Four 2.2 mm non-plated locating holes: (3,3), (49,3), (3,49), (49,49).

IMPORT AND FABRICATION
JLCEDA Professional: Import JLCEDA (Standard), select
Nightlight-RevK-JLCEDA-Standard.zip and extract its embedded libraries.
JLCEDA Standard: open Nightlight-Schematic.json and Nightlight-PCB.json.
Import as a new project. Synchronize the schematic and PCB, inspect changes,
and run native ERC/DRC. Native Channel IDs may need assignment after import.
Review solder mask and silkscreen, export Gerber/drill files, and inspect the
fabrication preview. The project ZIP is not a Gerber fabrication ZIP.

FRONT-SIDE MODULE SOLDERING
U1 uses exposed side-solder lands. U2 and U3 have closed module holes and use
short formed leads to exposed carrier lands. They do not use carrier through
holes. Both the module joint and carrier joint are soldered from the front.
Use ten individually formed, approximately 0.5 mm diameter tinned solid-wire
links, five per sensor. Their paths must not cross neighboring contacts.
Support modules at the specified height while soldering. Do not substitute a
straight through-hole header or bury unsoldered joints beneath the modules.

SUPERMINI U1
Body 18 x 22.4 mm at (27.4,21). Component face outward; USB-C toward top.
Module thickness 4.0 mm; nominal underside solder separation 0.2 mm.
Hole rows x=28.4 and 44.4 mm, pitch 2.54 mm. USB-end hole row y=22.6 mm.
Carrier pads 3.6 x 1.5 mm, extending 0.8 mm beyond each module side edge.
Front-view LEFT row, top to bottom:
GPIO5, GPIO6, GPIO7, GPIO8, GPIO9, GPIO10, GPIO20, GPIO21.
Front-view RIGHT row:
5V, GND, 3V3, GPIO4, GPIO3, GPIO2, GPIO1, GPIO0.
Pin numbering is retained with the module rotated 180 degrees in the PCB plane.
USB-C projects 1.3 mm beyond the upper module edge, within the carrier outline.
Program before final assembly; verify the chosen USB-C cable clears nearby
components before soldering U1. For later wired programming,
ensure the USB-C connector is accessible. Disconnect USB-A first.

RADAR U2
LD2410C nominal body 22 x 16 mm at (3.4,7.5), antenna face outward.
Module holes x=9.32 + n*2.54, y=9.0 mm. Left to right: TX, RX, OUT, GND, VCC.
Carrier lands are above the module at the same x positions, y=5.8 mm.
Mounting holes clear the full module-and-land bounding rectangle by >=0.75 mm.
Case supports clear the full radar outline by >=2.0 mm in plan view.
The entire underside component envelope remains reserved beneath the radar.
Hi-Link manual V1.00 specifies 2.54 mm pitch and 0.9 mm module holes.
The 1.5 mm hole-row edge offset is approximate physical measurement.
Module PCB underside: 1.8 mm above carrier front. This allows 1.5 mm underside
component projection plus 0.3 mm clearance. PCB thickness 1.6 mm; front
components project 0.9 mm. Antenna plane is 3.4 mm above carrier front.
Form leads outward over the upper module edge. Keep their upper envelope
within 4.75 mm of the carrier front; do not run leads across the antenna.

LIGHT SENSOR U3
GY-302 body 18.7 x 14.2 mm at (3.4,27). Component face outward, header RIGHT.
The module is rotated 180 degrees relative to the earlier left-header layout.
Right-hand module holes: x=21.1 mm; top-to-bottom VCC, GND, SCL, SDA, ADDR;
y=29.2 + n*2.54 mm. VCC edge margin 2.20 mm; ADDR margin 1.84 mm.
Carrier lands are at x=23.5 mm with matching y positions.
Use 0.3 mm underside separation; module total thickness is 2.1 mm.
Form leads outward over the right edge, maximum envelope 2.95 mm above carrier.
The sensor centre is approximately 6.8 mm from the left board edge; the case
aperture spans the module height to accommodate its other unspecified coordinate.
U3 uses +5V input and its onboard regulator/3.3V I2C pull-ups as before.

USB J1
Rear shell centre (26,26), cross-section 12 x 4.5 mm.
Solder-end reference-photo orientation matches the PCB front projection:
left outer contact +5V at (22.4,26); right outer contact GND at (29.6,26).
Power lands: 1.8 x 2.6 mm. Grounded shell lands: 3 x 3 mm, centres (19,26)
and (33,26). Straighten and fit shell legs to the broad exposed lands.
Trim D-/D+ contacts. Rear copper and vias are excluded from x=24..28,
y=24.3..27.7 mm. Front-only SMD copper may lie beneath this region, separated
from the trimmed contacts by the 1.6 mm carrier. Trimmed tails must not lift
the connector or extend outside the reserved rear region.
Rear non-ground copper clears the shell-rim contact bands by at least 0.20 mm.
Preserve those bands when editing. Rear copper-fill cutouts mark the rim.
Board-rear-surface to tip: 18.7..18.8 mm. Case projection: 15.7..15.8 mm.

LED CIRCUIT
Three independent channels are retained: GPIO10, GPIO0 and GPIO1.
Each uses an AO3400A, 470R LED resistor, 220R gate resistor and 10k pulldown.
LED bodies and land patterns remain the existing 3.20 x 2.47 mm design.
Physical LED fit has not been measured. LED centres are (14.5,46.735), (26,46.735), (37.5,46.735) mm,
with equal 11.5 mm horizontal spacing and symmetric side margins.
All original electrical pin/net assignments are retained; physical positions
and module orientations are defined by this revision's footprint map.

CHECKS AND LIMITS
check_placement.py checks pad placement, copper reservations and board edges.
validate.py checks serialized native connectivity, all pin/net assignments,
0.20 mm copper clearance, 0.30 mm drill clearance, measured module geometry,
the front-side assembly constraint, and rear USB contact reservations.
These checks do not replace native ERC/DRC, mask review or physical assembly.
The ESP antenna envelope, radar row offset and hand-formed lead shapes remain
approximate. RF performance, optical isolation and printed fit are unqualified.
